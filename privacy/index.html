Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

This policy covers the apps I publish on the App Store. It says what each app collects, what the data is used for, and what choices you have.

The short version

  • An app collects only what it needs to work.
  • Your data is never sold.
  • Each app is listed below with exactly what it collects.

What each app collects

No apps are published yet. When one is, it will be listed here with the data it collects, the reason, and where the data is stored.

Data Apple collects

Apple handles App Store downloads and purchases. Apple may share crash reports and usage numbers with me if you turned that on in your device settings. Those reports do not identify you. Apple's own privacy policy covers that data.

Sharing

Your data is not sold or rented. It is shared only when the law requires it, or with a service an app needs to run. Any such service is named in that app's entry above.

Children

These apps are not aimed at children under 13, and they do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete any data an app holds about you. Use the contact on the support page.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date.

Questions about privacy? See the support page.
